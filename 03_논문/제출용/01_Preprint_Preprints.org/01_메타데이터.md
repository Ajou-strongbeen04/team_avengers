## Abstract (≤ 200 words)

> `../../논문_초안_v2.md` §0 초록을 v3 기준으로 갱신해 영역(英譯). v3 변경점: "2계층 ROM" → "CFD-generated POD basis constrains LLM inference to a divergence-free space".

```
[TBD — 아래 문단 구조로 작성]

(1) Problem: single-point DO sensors cannot see spatial oxygen distribution;
    full CFD is neither real-time on consumer devices nor free of per-case
    manual boundary-condition setup.
(2) Approach: a single photograph → VLM geometry/topology recognition →
    normalized boundary conditions (JSON) → physics-basis-constrained LLM
    inference of POD coefficients → divergence-free flow reconstruction →
    advection–diffusion → coarse (4×9) hypoxia risk map.
(3) Honesty layer: closure discrepancies are lumped into an effective oxygen
    transfer coefficient identified from an unsteady re-aeration test;
    residuals propagate to a probabilistic output.
(4) Results: [TBD — EX1 size sweep, EX2 re-aeration κ, EX3 5-fold CV top1/top1-Q]
(5) Position: a dashboard warning light, not autonomous control.
```
